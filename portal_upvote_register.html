Security

Built without compromise.

Scoped permissions
The session key can only call the Portal upvote contract. It cannot transfer funds, access your wallet, or interact with any other protocol.
Encrypted at rest
Your session key is AES-256 encrypted before storage. It never exists unencrypted on our servers at any point in the process.
Self-expiring
Every session key expires automatically after 30 days. Renew only when you want to continue. There is no auto-renewal.
